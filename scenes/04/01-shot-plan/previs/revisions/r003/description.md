## Blocking and edit

In the Edirne Palace workroom at night, the south-end wide establishes the long lamp-lit table, the bombard drawing and the cool windows on the east wall. Mehmed circles from the far end to the west side. Urban waits opposite and Mara stays back near the windows. At 3.55 seconds, cut across the table to Mehmed as he asks about Constantinople. Reverse at 7.05 seconds as Urban leans toward the drawing and indicates successive points while explaining how the cannon wears down the wall and its repair crews.

At 14.95 seconds, bring both men and the parchment back into frame for their exchange about time. Mehmed signals the scribe. At 21.7 seconds, cut to the chest and the near edge of the table as the scribe opens the lid, revealing the coins. Return to Mehmed at 24.4 seconds for the offer. Cut to Urban at 30.1 seconds for his question about bronze and back to Mehmed at 33.25 seconds for the answer about bells.

At 38.3 seconds, frame Urban in the foreground with Mara behind him and ease closer. She holds her gaze on him as his eyes drop to the drawing. Stay on that reaction through the end at 42 seconds.

## Dialogue timing

- **3.83–6.67 s · Mehmed II** — Can it break the walls of Constantinople?
- **7.29–14.71 s · Urban** — No cannon breaks a wall by itself. It breaks one stone. Then another. Then the sleep of the men repairing them.
- **15.54–17.83 s · Mehmed II** — So it is a weapon against time.
- **18.46–20.71 s · Urban** — Against time, pride, and mortar.
- **24.83–28.96 s · Mehmed II** — You asked the Romans for a price. I will pay four.
- **30.58–31.96 s · Urban** — And the bronze?
- **33.58–37.54 s · Mehmed II** — My empire has bells enough to become thunder.
